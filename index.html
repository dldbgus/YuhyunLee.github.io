About

Hi! I'm Yuhyun Lee, a Ph.D. candidate in Biomedical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Prof. Pulkit Grover. My work brings together experimental neuroscience and engineering to develop and evaluate neural interfaces.

My doctoral research focuses on transnasal electrical stimulation in mice, combining nasal electrode arrays with depth-resolved electrophysiology to understand stimulation-evoked responses across brain regions.

Previously, I developed EEG recording systems for adult and larval zebrafish. I am particularly interested in research that connects preclinical platforms, neural signal analysis, and translational neurotechnology.

DOCTORAL RESEARCH

Transnasal stimulation & depth-resolved neural recording

Developed an in vivo mouse platform combining a 32-site nasal stimulation array and 64-channel intracerebral recordings to characterize stimulation-evoked activity across recording depths. Current work examines response timing, stimulation parameters, and anatomical correspondence—not yet selective deep-brain activation.

Project details →

PUBLISHED · JNE 2026

Why electric-field orientation matters

Designed orthogonal stimulation configurations for cortical brain slices and paired neural recordings with FEM modeling. Observed activation thresholds more than fivefold lower for out-of-plane fields than for fields aligned with cortical columns.

Project details → · Read journal paper

MASTER'S RESEARCH

Scalable EEG platforms for zebrafish

Built multi-animal EEG systems for adult and larval zebrafish using mechanical design and microfluidics. Validated recording performance in seizure and anti-epileptic treatment models. Related device inventions received registrations in Korea and the EU.

Project details → · Read journal paper

CROSS-DISCIPLINARY

Connecting electric-field models with physiology

Use FEM simulations, electrode configurations, and experimental response measurements to reason about stimulation efficacy and model limitations. Contributed to preclinical evaluation of transnasal DeepFocus stimulation.
